### Selection rules

- One page owns one narrow intent group. Close variants belong to that page.
- Informational articles do not target the existing commercial term
  `automated SEO reports`.
- Agency software terms belong to a future use-case page, not a blog article.
- MCP, Claude, database-reactivation and branded-tool queries were excluded
  because they do not match the current product.
- Each article links its diagnosis to RankCues' actual evidence workflow without
  claiming unavailable free-tool functionality.

### SERP outline findings

`tools to detect content decay`

- Ranking pages use definitions, tool comparisons, causes, detection metrics,
  refresh tactics and FAQs.
- RankCues differentiates with sustained-decline thresholds, false-positive
  checks, a decision table and a fixed post-refresh verification window.

`keyword cannibalization audit`

- Ranking pages cover definition, GSC/site-search detection, redirects,
  canonicalization, merging, differentiation and prevention.
- RankCues owns the narrower idea that shared keywords are not enough: URL
  switching, wrong-page wins and duplicated intent are the audit triggers.

`internal linking audit tool`

- Results mix full-site crawlers, single-page analyzers, CMS assistants and
  enterprise platforms. Recurring sections cover crawl depth, orphans, anchor
  text, broken links and link opportunities.
- RankCues adds an evidence schema for every recommendation and re-crawl plus
  28-56 day outcome verification.

`GSC vs GA4 data discrepancies`

- Results focus on clicks versus sessions, timezone, consent/blockers,
  canonical URLs, channel scope, attribution and reporting delays.
- RankCues leads with a ratio step-change diagnostic and explicitly assigns
  search visibility to GSC and on-site outcomes to GA4.

`SEO change tracking`

- Results emphasize alerts, HTML/metadata changes, history, page segments,
  rank tracking and tool comparisons.
- RankCues separates detection, correlation and measurement, then adds a frozen
  baseline, confounders and a result/revert record.

### Cannibalization guardrails

- `/features/automated-seo-reports` remains the owner of automated reporting.
- `/resources/content-decay-detection-tools` may mention refresh tools but does
  not target a future product page for `content refresh tool SEO`.
- `/resources/internal-linking-audit-tool` is a selection/method guide; a future
  interactive tool should use a `/tools/` URL and a transactional title.
- `/resources/seo-change-tracking` owns change monitoring education. A future
  feature page should target `SEO change intelligence software` and link back
  to this guide rather than duplicate its definition sections.
