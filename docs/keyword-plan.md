RankCues differentiation:

- Lead with causal investigation and change context, not PDF generation.
- Demonstrate page/CMS/deployment events next to GSC and GA4 anomalies.
- Label detected facts, correlations and hypotheses separately.
- Make the weekly report a reviewable output of the investigation workflow.
